Questions

Straight answers.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of pages and what you need. We give you a clear price in writing before any final work starts, and it is a one-time payment.

Are there monthly fees?

No. You pay once for your website. Your domain name is renewed yearly with the domain registrar, and we tell you the cost upfront.

Do I own the website?

Yes. Once it is paid for, the website and your domain are yours.

How long does it take?

Usually within about a week once we have your business details, services and photos.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. Send us the link and we will tell you honestly whether it needs a rebuild or just some fixes.

What do you need from me?

Your business name, services, opening hours, contact details, your logo if you have one, and a few photos.
